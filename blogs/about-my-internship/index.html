About my Internship

I ended my 6-month internship at LSEG. It was one of the most positive and memorable experiences of my life. I met a lot of friends from all stages of their careers and a lot of very friendly and helpful people, also super knowledgeable and impressively technical.

I went in with no experience in corporate culture and having never worked in such an environment. My team (shoutout to LSE Team C – the best!) was incredibly welcoming and I never felt like an outsider from day 1. All the team members were very kind and helpful which made me calm my nerves down.

Internship started off with 4 days of orientation where we had sessions about everything from financial markets to AI at LSEG to corporate culture at LSEG. They were very helpful to get an idea of the company and what they do and what I am collaborating for. The sessions were there throughout the internship, along fireside chats and a meet and greet with Head of Engineering.

Me and two other interns (all SE) were tasked with converting tests from Fortress1 internal testing framework to Fortress2. We had KT sessions which explained us the architecture of the London Stock Exchange system and each of the components and the messaging structure. And there were a lot of very knowledgeable people always around us so that we could solve any problem we had instantly. We wrote tests, we converted tests using an AI framework, we wrote validation profiles and filter profiles and sometimes even changed some of the core code on fortress architecture which was super exciting.

I was tasked with another task towards the middle of my internship. To write a tool that made snapshots of orderbooks by reading the logs. It was given to me by Mr. Suvin Ranasinghe, an associate architect. He wanted me to automate a task he did manually. He explained everything perfectly and clarified any follow up questions I had. It was a super exciting task because I was building something from scratch, I was writing logic. I wrote that code and then did some optimizations as well, using C++ features and data structures. Then I gave it a go using Python and surprisingly it ran even faster. (I didn’t know about compiler optimizations in C++, even though I did some pthread parallel programming in the code, Or it could be how Python handles I/O). I finished that task and then did further modifications to modularized it and even run it with a JSON config file.

Enough about work, outside of work, it was even more memorable. Coffee time chats, team lunches, lunch outs (which I learned are very difficult to plan) and playing table tennis sometimes were just the best times. We had a tournament as well. Consisting of Foosball, TT and Badminton, and the final was full of energy and we planned a “Paduru Party” with food and music afterwards. And this was the day before the last day of my internship, great way to end it if you ask me.

Well, before I end it, I should mention few of the names (I’m sorry I couldn’t mention everyone), first of all my people leader and senior tech lead Mr. Hishara Mudalige. He was super helpful from day 1, very approachable and would always give me a solution and would work with me to figure it out whenever I had a problem. I strive to one day be a tech lead like my first tech lead. Mrs. Sreeni Dissanayake, an associate architect. She also worked together with me to figure things out and was also very approachable, she also helped me a lot while working. Mr. Suvin Ranasinghe, whom I mentioned before was also one of the kindest people I’ve met. Coming from UCSC, it was inspiring to see past alumni doing so well at the industry. Next the senior and specialist software engineers, they were the most approachable, being closer to our age but they were also very knowledgeable and were always there for us. Mr. Anupa Satharasinghe who taught me the art of debugging (I’ve never used the IntelliJ debugger before) and taught me what a great skill it was to have in my arsenal. Mr. Rasika Doranepola, Mr. Pasindu Ranathunga, Mr. Samal Punsara, Mr. Vinuja Ranasinghe and many more for being there for us throughout the way. Also shout out to my fellow interns (Exchange Interns as we call ourselves), Sakith, Dilara, Mindi and Murshid, it was amazing because of you all. Thank you!
