IEEEXtreme 19.0 – What a ride!

We created the team WeForgetComments in our first year and IEEEXtreme was our first competitive programming hackathon. We instantly fell in love with it, tackling programming problems for 24 hours, fueled by the almighty coffee machine in the hall. We spent time coming up with solutions together and witnessed how the best teams tackled them. Even though we got absolutely destroyed (did not even make the top 100 from the country), we were content with our efforts and vowed to come back and achieve our goal year.

Fast forward to IEEEXtreme 18.0, we know more than we did on our second year (Going from C to C++ was like cavemen discovering fire, no it was like discovering a smart phone). We got to our initial goal of finishing in the top 100 of the country. We finished 46th in the country, 4th in the university. We got the ambitious idea to be the best of the best, seeing our batchmates become 2nd in the country and get the global rank of 75. We vowed to come back once again and get a global 100 finish.

Now we’re in our third year and it’s time to have a go at it again. Here comes IEEEXtreme 19.0. We do our best and start off really strong (at midway we were the global 2nd). Then the tougher problems came and we slowed down but kept going at it. Eventually finishing 57th globally. 1st nationally but IEEE had the brilliant idea of only sending our global rank in the certificate and not mention the country rank anywhere. We felt like we achieved our goals. Figured out we’d be too busy on our final year and that closed the glorious chapter of IEEEXtreme.
