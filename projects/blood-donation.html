Drops of Hope

A web platform that brings donors, recipients, and blood banks into the same workflow.

  • Next.js
  • Node.js
  • Express
  • TypeScript
  • PostgreSQL
  • Prisma
  • Asgardeo
  • Supabase
View source

Coordinating appointments

Donors need a way to schedule a donation. Blood banks need a way to manage those appointments alongside incoming requests. The project brings those workflows together so that appointment information can support day-to-day planning.

Looking ahead at demand

The forecasting component addresses a separate question from appointment scheduling. It helps blood banks anticipate how much blood may be needed in the future.
