Research · 2025–26

Cloud-native fault tolerance

My final-year research looks at how Kubernetes-based microservices recover after a failure, and how to shorten the time users spend waiting for the service to work again.

  • Kubernetes
  • Observability
  • Recovery time

The question

A container can restart before the application can successfully serve requests again. If recovery is measured at the container, that gap is easy to miss. I’m interested in when the user-facing service becomes usable again and which parts of the recovery process account for the wait.

What I’m exploring

The project explores adaptive recovery mechanisms for containerized services. Comparing recovery approaches means observing what happens inside the cluster alongside the responses a user would receive. The time between the first failure and successful requests is the measure I’m interested in.
